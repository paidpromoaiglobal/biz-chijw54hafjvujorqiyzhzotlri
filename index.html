Our Story

Made with Passion

Nasar Biriyani Centre & Catering Event Biriyani is a beloved culinary destination located in the heart of Old Washermanpet, Chennai, on Thiruvottiyur High Road. We take immense pride in serving authentic, flavour-packed biriyani that honours the time-honoured recipes of Tamil Nadu. Whether you are stopping by for a quick satisfying meal or planning a grand event, our dedication to quality ingredients and perfect spice balance ensures an unforgettable dining experience every single time.

Beyond our restaurant, we specialise in catering services for events of all sizes — from intimate family gatherings to large-scale corporate and social celebrations. Our team is committed to delivering the same exceptional taste and freshness to your doorstep. Open seven days a week from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, we are always ready to serve you. Our perfect 5-star rating is a testament to the trust and love our customers place in us with every order.
